## Executive Summary of Recorded Decision

On 2026-10-26, the project owner formally selected **Option A: Focused Food Control Scope**.

### 1. Scope Boundary
- **Selected Option:** Option A — Focused Food Control Scope.
- **Corpus Focus:** Authentic Sri Lankan Food Control and Labelling regulations, prioritizing authentic Gazettes, amendment/version relationships, and compositional/preservative/flavouring/sugar schedules.
- **Benchmark Minimum:** At least 25 distinct eligible clauses and at least 100 scenarios (150 stretch target).
- **Statistical Validity:** Satisfies Proposal Section 10.3 clause-level cluster bootstrap requirement with $N \ge 25$ independent clause clusters.

### 2. Candidate Regulatory Sources
The candidate sources identified for this scope are:
1. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2494/47** (Food Labelling and Advertising Regulations 2026) — Promulgated, authentic (25 clauses segmented in DB).
2. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2494/06** (Food Colour Coding for Sugar Levels Amendment Regulations 2026) — Promulgated, authentic (4 clauses segmented in DB).
3. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2113/16** (Food Preservatives Regulations 2019) — Promulgated, authentic (8 clauses segmented in DB).
4. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 1795/51** (Food Flavouring Substances and Flavour Enhancers Regulations 2013) — Promulgated, authentic (7 clauses segmented in DB).
5. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2443/27** (Food Labelling and Advertising Amendment Regulations 2025) — Promulgated, authentic amendment chain to 2494/47 (4 clauses segmented in DB).
6. **Gazette Extraordinary No. 1688/28** (Food Colouring Substances Regulations 2011) — Authentic scanned raster PDF, subject to Tesseract OCR and visual page verification before clause ingestion.

*Note: These are candidates, not automatically approved sources or eligible clauses. Each clause must be individually verified and reviewed.*

### 3. Explicit Exclusions
- Central Environmental Authority (CEA) Environmental Protection Licensing regulations are **excluded** from this bounded scope.
- Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) and Employees' Trust Fund (ETF) labour regulations are **excluded** from this bounded scope.
- Neither domain will be introduced unless the project owner later formally issues an explicit directive amending this record.

The frozen Proposal v4 (`RegImpact-SL_Project_Proposal_v4_FINAL.md`) is strictly preserved without modification. Option A operationally interprets the research commitment as governed by the clause-level and benchmark targets of Section 6, Section 9.2, and Section 11 Priority Rule: achieving $\ge 25$ distinct operative clauses and $\ge 100$ scenarios across authentic Food Control regulations with high provenance and paired scenario integrity.
